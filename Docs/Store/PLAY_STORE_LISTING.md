# Beyond The Beat — Google Play Store Listing Draft

## App title

Beyond The Beat

## Short description

Drive, explore, complete missions, and discover an offline open world.

## Full description

Beyond The Beat is an offline open-world driving and exploration game built around missions, free-roam activities, and changing environments.

Take control of a vehicle using mobile-first touch controls, explore connected regions, complete different mission types, and continue roaming after objectives are finished.

Current gameplay includes:

- Driving, parking, and contextual ACTION interactions.
- Reach Location missions across the open world.
- Forest survival objectives with a lightweight resource system.
- Restricted-area missions that combine navigation with a reusable puzzle gate.
- Free-roam activities including cooking, repair, and a mechanic job with credit rewards.
- Ocean exploration with swimming, diving, surface controls, and discoverable checkpoints.
- Local progress persistence for supported mission and exploration state.
- A first-launch tutorial for the core mobile controls.

Beyond The Beat is designed as a focused offline mobile experience. There is no online multiplayer requirement for the current game loop.

## Screenshot capture storyboard

Store screenshots must be captured from the actual Android build. Do not use generated mock gameplay or device-frame composites.

Capture at least these five landscape scenes where practical:

1. **Driving / core controls** — vehicle moving through the world with LEFT, RIGHT, GO, REV, and ACTION visible.
2. **Forest survival** — player inside the forest survival zone with mission progress/resource UI readable.
3. **Restricted-area puzzle** — puzzle/gate state visible while the Reach + Solve mission is active.
4. **Ocean exploration** — swimming or diving with DIVE/SURFACE controls and exploration progress visible.
5. **Free-roam mechanic activity** — repair/mechanic job state with the mechanic HUD and credit reward context visible.

For Google Play game-promotion eligibility, target at least three 16:9 landscape screenshots at **1920x1080 or higher**. The minimum publishing requirement is at least two screenshots. Keep each screenshot between 320px and 3840px, with the longest dimension no more than twice the shortest dimension.

## Graphic asset requirements used by this repository

- Play Store app icon: **512x512**, 32-bit PNG with alpha, maximum **1024 KB**.
- Feature graphic: **1024x500**, JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha.
- Phone screenshots: at least **2** required to publish; actual in-game captures only for this project.
- Game recommendation target: at least **3** 16:9 landscape screenshots at **1920x1080+**.
- Do not add ranking, pricing, review, award, or misleading promotional claims to the icon, feature graphic, title, or descriptions.
